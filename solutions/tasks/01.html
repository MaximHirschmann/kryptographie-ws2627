Question

Provide a formal definition of the $\mathsf{Gen}$, $\mathsf{Enc}$, and $\mathsf{Dec}$ algorithms for both the mono-alphabetic substitution and Vigenère ciphers. Remark: a permutation is typically denoted $\pi$.

Setup

Notation as in the lecture (script §2.2 and Mitschrift): $\ell$ denotes the number of permutations, i.e. the size of the key space, not the size of the alphabet.

Let $\Sigma = \{0, \dots, 25\}$ be the alphabet ($\texttt{a} = 0, \dots, \texttt{z} = 25$). A plaintext is a word $m = m_0 m_1 \cdots m_{|m|-1} \in \Sigma^*$ and is encrypted letter by letter. As in the script, $c = c_0 c_1 \cdots c_{|c|-1}$ is the ciphertext.

Mono-alphabetic substitution cipher

Let $\{\pi_0, \dots, \pi_{\ell - 1}\}$ be the set of all permutations $\pi : \Sigma \to \Sigma$, so $\ell = |\Sigma|! = 26!$. The key is the index of a permutation:

$$\mathcal{K} = \{0, \dots, \ell - 1\}, \qquad \ell = 26! \approx 2^{88}.$$
Gen $k \leftarrow \{0, \dots, \ell - 1\}$ uniformly output $k$
Enc input $k \in \mathcal{K}$, $m \in \Sigma^*$ for $i = 0$ to $|m| - 1$ do $c_i := \pi_k(m_i)$ output $c = c_0 \cdots c_{|m|-1}$
Dec input $k \in \mathcal{K}$, $c \in \Sigma^*$ for $i = 0$ to $|c| - 1$ do $m_i := \pi_k^{-1}(c_i)$ output $m = m_0 \cdots m_{|c|-1}$

Correctness: $\mathsf{Dec}_k(\mathsf{Enc}_k(m))_i = \pi_k^{-1}(\pi_k(m_i)) = \mathrm{id}(m_i) = m_i$ for every position $i$.

Careful with $\ell$: it is $26!$, not $26$. If $\ell$ were the alphabet size, $\{0, \dots, \ell - 1\}$ would only index 26 permutations, e.g. only the shifts.

Vigenère cipher

As in the script, the Vigenère cipher applies $n$ shift ciphers in turn, where $n$ is the key length. The shift by $k$ is the permutation $x \mapsto x + k \bmod 26$, so the $i$-th letter is encrypted with the key letter $k_{i \bmod n}$.

$$\mathcal{K} = \Sigma^n = \{0, \dots, 25\}^n.$$
Gen for $i = 0$ to $n - 1$ do $k_i \leftarrow \{0, \dots, 25\}$ uniformly  (= $\mathsf{Gen}$ of the shift cipher) output $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{n-1})$
Enc input $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{n-1}) \in \mathcal{K}$, $m \in \Sigma^*$ for $i = 0$ to $|m| - 1$ do $c_i := m_i + k_{i \bmod n} \bmod 26$ output $c = c_0 \cdots c_{|m|-1}$
Dec input $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{n-1}) \in \mathcal{K}$, $c \in \Sigma^*$ for $i = 0$ to $|c| - 1$ do $m_i := c_i - k_{i \bmod n} \bmod 26$ output $m = m_0 \cdots m_{|c|-1}$

Correctness: $\mathsf{Dec}_k(\mathsf{Enc}_k(m))_i = (m_i + k_{i \bmod n}) - k_{i \bmod n} = m_i \bmod 26$ for every position $i$.

If each $k_i$ were a whole permutation from the mono-alphabetic $\mathsf{Gen}$ (so $c_i := \pi_{k_{i \bmod n}}(m_i)$), we would get a general polyalphabetic substitution cipher. Vigenère is the special case where every $\pi_{k_i}$ is a shift.

Example (from the script)

$$ \begin{array}{ll} \text{plaintext} & \texttt{tellhimaboutme} \\ \text{keyword} & \texttt{cafecafecafeca} \\ \text{ciphertext} & \texttt{WFRQKJSFEPAYPF} \end{array} $$
