Question

Compute $[171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \bmod 35]$ (by hand).

Answer. $[171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \bmod 35] = 26$.

Step 1: Reduce the base

$$171 = 4 \cdot 35 + 31 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 171 \equiv 31 \equiv -4 \pmod{35}.$$

$\gcd(31, 35) = 1$, so $31 \in \mathbb{Z}_{35}^*$ and Euler's theorem applies (07_number_theory, Theorem 1.26). Its Corollary 1.27 says exactly what we need: $a^x \bmod n = a^{x \bmod \varphi(n)} \bmod n$ for $(a, n) = 1$.

Step 2: Reduce the exponent

$\varphi(35) = \varphi(5)\varphi(7) = 4 \cdot 6 = 24$, so $a^{24} \equiv 1 \pmod{35}$ for $a \in \mathbb{Z}_{35}^*$, and the exponent can be reduced modulo $24$:

$$4{,}800{,}000{,}047 = 4{,}800{,}000{,}000 + 47, \qquad 4{,}800{,}000{,}000 = 48 \cdot 10^8 = 24 \cdot 2 \cdot 10^8 \equiv 0, \qquad 47 \equiv 23 \pmod{24}.$$ $$\Longrightarrow\quad 171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \equiv 31^{23} \pmod{35}.$$

Step 3: $31^{23} = 31^{-1}$

$31^{23} \cdot 31 = 31^{24} \equiv 1$, so $31^{23} \equiv 31^{-1} \pmod{35}$. We need $x$ with $-4x \equiv 1 \pmod{35}$:

$$4 \cdot 26 = 104 = 3 \cdot 35 - 1 \equiv -1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad (-4) \cdot 26 \equiv 1 \pmod{35}.$$

Check: $31 \cdot 26 = 806 = 23 \cdot 35 + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{35}$. ✓

$$171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \equiv 26 \pmod{35}.$$

Alternative: with the Carmichael function $\lambda(35) = \mathrm{lcm}(4, 6) = 12$, the exponent becomes $4{,}800{,}000{,}047 \equiv 11 \pmod{12}$, so $31^{11} = 31^{-1} = 26$ as well. Or compute modulo 5 and 7 separately and combine with the CRT: $171 \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ gives $1$, and $171 \equiv 3 \pmod 7$ with exponent $\equiv 5 \pmod 6$ gives $3^5 = 243 \equiv 5$. The number that is $\equiv 1 \pmod 5$ and $\equiv 5 \pmod 7$ is $26$.
