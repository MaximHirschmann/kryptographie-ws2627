Question

Compute $[171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \bmod 35]$ (by hand).

Answer. $[171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \bmod 35] = 26$.

Use the Chinese remainder theorem: compute modulo $5$ and modulo $7$ separately, reducing each exponent with Fermat's little theorem.

Modulo 5

$$171 \equiv 1 \pmod 5 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \equiv 1 \pmod 5.$$

Modulo 7

$171 = 24 \cdot 7 + 3$, so $171 \equiv 3 \pmod 7$. Exponents can be reduced modulo $6$, and $4{,}800{,}000{,}047 = 6 \cdot 800{,}000{,}007 + 5$:

$$171^{4{,}800{,}000{,}047} \equiv 3^5 = 243 = 34 \cdot 7 + 5 \equiv 5 \pmod 7.$$

Combine

We need $x \in \{0, \dots, 34\}$ with $x \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ and $x \equiv 5 \pmod 7$. The candidates $\equiv 5 \pmod 7$ are $5, 12, 19, 26, \dots$; the first one $\equiv 1 \pmod 5$ is $26$. $\blacksquare$
